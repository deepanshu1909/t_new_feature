    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Why do I register to see full details?

    Fees, dates and eligibility change often. Registering once lets a counsellor confirm the latest figures and call you with them, and it unlocks full details, shortlist and compare.

    Is GetYourCollege an official college website?

    No. We provide independent guidance and are not an official site of any college listed here.

    Do I need an account to shortlist colleges?

    No account. After you register, your shortlist is saved in this browser. Clearing your browser data clears it.

    Is the guidance free?

    Yes. Asking for guidance does not cost you anything.

    Who sees my details?

    GetYourCollege and the college(s) named above the submit button. See our Terms & Privacy.

    Still have questions?

    Talk to a counsellor. It is free and takes a few minutes.

    Not sure where to start?

    Tell us what you want to study and where. We will help you narrow it down.
